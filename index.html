Who This Is For

Is This the Right Time to Reach Out?

Built for patients and families seriously considering treatment in India — wherever you are in that decision.

This may be the right step for you if:

  • You or a family member has a new cancer diagnosis
  • A biopsy, scan or blood report is available or on the way
  • Surgery, chemotherapy, radiation or another treatment has been recommended
  • You're exploring options after treatment has already started in Bangladesh
  • The cancer has returned after earlier treatment
  • You want a specialist's view before deciding on the next step
  • You're comparing cancer hospitals in India and want clearer information
  • A bone marrow / stem cell transplant (BMT) has been recommended
  • The diagnosis is leukaemia, lymphoma or multiple myeloma
  • The diagnosis is aplastic anaemia or another serious blood disorder
  • A transplant opinion has already been received and you want a second view
  • HLA typing or donor evaluation has started
  • You are considering a bone marrow transplant in India and want to understand cost and hospitals
  • You want a haematology/BMT team to review the case before planning travel
  • CABG / heart bypass surgery has been recommended
  • Valve repair or valve replacement has been recommended
  • A complex cardiac procedure is being considered
  • An angiogram or echo is available for specialist review
  • You are comparing heart surgery options and cardiac hospitals in India
  • You want a cardiac surgeon's view and an indicative cost before travel
Not sure which specialist or treatment pathway is relevant? If you have a diagnosis, medical recommendation or reports and are considering treatment in India, share the case and our care companion can guide you on the appropriate next step.

What changes the cost
