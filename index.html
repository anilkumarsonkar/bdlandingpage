Cancer Treatment in India

Cancer Treatment Options Available in India Through Our Hospital Network

India has a wide range of specialised cancer-care capabilities, with multidisciplinary teams and advanced treatment options available across established hospitals. Depending on the diagnosis and clinical requirements, treatment may include:

Cancer Surgery

Surgical treatment for cancers where removal of the tumour or affected tissue is recommended.

Chemotherapy

Drug-based treatment used for different types and stages of cancer.

Radiation Therapy

Advanced radiation techniques used to target cancer cells while aiming to protect surrounding healthy tissue.

Immunotherapy

Treatment that works with the body's immune system to help fight certain cancers.

Targeted Therapy

Medicines designed to target specific characteristics of cancer cells.

Bone Marrow / Stem Cell Transplant

Specialised treatment for selected blood cancers and other conditions requiring stem-cell transplantation.

CAR T-Cell Therapy

A cellular immunotherapy that re-engineers the patient's own T-cells, used for selected blood cancers at specialised centres.

Treatment options depend on the patient's diagnosis, cancer type, stage, overall health and specialist assessment. Final treatment decisions are made by the treating medical team.
