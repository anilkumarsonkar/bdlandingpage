Who This Is For

Is This the Right Time to Reach Out?

This service is built for patients and families who are seriously considering treatment in India — wherever you are in that decision.

This may be the right step for you if:

  • You or a family member has a new cancer diagnosis
  • A biopsy, scan, or blood report is available or on the way
  • Surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, or another treatment has been recommended
  • You're exploring options after treatment has already started in Bangladesh
  • The cancer has returned after earlier treatment
  • You want a specialist's view before deciding on the next step
  • You're comparing hospitals or countries and want clearer information
Not sure if this applies to your situation? Reach out anyway — a coordinator can help you understand if this is the right next step, with no obligation.

Cost

How Much Does Cancer Treatment in India Cost?

There's no single answer — but there is a clear way to get yours.

Cancer treatment cost varies from case to case. It depends on:

  • The diagnosis and stage
  • Any treatment already received
  • The specific procedure or treatment approach required
  • The hospital and specialist involved

Because of this, GlobalCare doesn't publish a fixed price list — and you should be cautious of anyone who gives you a cancer treatment cost without reviewing your reports first.

Instead, once your reports are reviewed, GlobalCare will help you understand an indicative treatment estimate for your specific case, so you can plan with real information rather than a guess.
