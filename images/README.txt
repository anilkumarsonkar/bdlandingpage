IMAGES FOLDER
=============

This landing page currently loads images from Unsplash CDN URLs as PLACEHOLDERS
(hero, treatments, hospitals, doctors, testimonials, "why us"). They are marked
in index.html with comments like:  [Replace with verified data].

Before going live, replace them with LICENSED, brand-approved images and update
the <img src="..."> paths to point to files in this folder, e.g.:

  images/hero-patient-doctor.jpg
  images/treatment-cardiac.jpg
  images/hospital-apollo.jpg
  images/doctor-1.jpg
  images/og-bangladesh-india.jpg   (1200 x 630 for social sharing)
  images/favicon.png

Recommended:
- Use your own licensed photography or a paid stock license.
- Optimize/compress images (WebP or well-compressed JPG) for fast loading.
- Add descriptive, keyword-relevant alt text (already scaffolded in the HTML).
- Do NOT use hospital/doctor photos you are not licensed to use.
